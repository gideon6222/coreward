# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 September 2026**

## The short version

The Lattice collects nothing about you and has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

## What is collected

Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account, or any other
personal information. It has no login, no leaderboard, no chat and no advertising.

## What leaves your device

The Lattice is a web game. The Android app is a thin wrapper that shows the game from its
website, https://gideon6222.github.io/lattice/, so your device fetches the game's files from
that website (hosted by GitHub Pages) the way a browser fetches any page. That is the only
network traffic the game makes. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising
SDK, no attribution SDK and no third-party service of any kind, and nothing you do in the game
is sent anywhere.

## What is stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally in the browser storage of the app, on your device. It never
leaves the device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions beyond those the operating system grants every app
by default. It does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, location, files or the
phone's identifiers.

## Children

The game collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If a future version ever adds something that collects or transmits data, this policy will be
updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in the in-game
changelog.
